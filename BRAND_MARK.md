# Bober Yeet War mark

Metaphor: **square beaver + a chunky wooden yeet-club at the chin** —
same Green Home chocolate-bar face, now armed with a bat, not loaded
in a sling. Clip-art polygons. The 32px read is **square beaver + stick**.

Shape: **chamfered chocolate-bar beaver HEAD** (flat wood face, not a
circle, not an iOS squircle tile) with two giant cream buckteeth hanging
off a cream snout. A **single fat wooden YEET STICK** (club / bat with a
handle bulge and a thicker barrel, two leather wraps) sits behind and
beside the chin — one shaft, no fork. Field is a **flat night purple**
sky, full-bleed square. No peaked roof, no ticket, no stars, no coin
logo, no sling-V.

Palette (4):
- Fur brown `#8B5A2B`
- Bucktooth cream `#F4E6C3`
- Stick / charge yellow `#F5C400`
- Night purple `#3A2A6A`
