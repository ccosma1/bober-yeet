# Bober Yeet mark

Metaphor: **Bober loaded in a yellow slingshot** — a cartoon beaver about
to get yeeted at wood and stone. Clip-art, not a coin logo, not a night-sky
desk app. The read at 32px is **square beaver + buckteeth + sling-V**.

Shape: **chunky squarish beaver head** (rounded chocolate-bar face, not a
circle, not an iOS squircle tile) with two giant cream buckteeth hanging
off a cream snout. A **tiny yellow sling-V** sits behind and below the
chin — two thick forks, no rubber-glow, no orb. Field is a **flat night
purple** sky (Uranus), full-bleed square. No peaked roof, no ticket, no
stars.

Palette (4):
- Fur brown `#8B5A2B`
- Bucktooth cream `#F4E6C3`
- Sling yellow `#F5C400`
- Night purple `#3A2A6A`

Forbidden (do not reuse):
- **Orion / grok-crypto-trader**: constellation, stars, sparkles, deep-space
  navy, cyan rim, nebula glow, stacked glow-orbs, rounded-square + inner
  glow-circle recipe
- **Lowlight**: iron lantern, peaked roof, keyhole glass, oxblood / amber
  lamp, street-post silhouette
- **ViaPass / VignetteRoute**: cream travel ticket, punch-holes, terracotta
  sun, teal visa stamp, landscape stub card
- **ConstructCost**: invoice / construction-cost clipart, calculator tiles
- **CMC / MultiversX Bober assets** in `assets/ref/`: do not paste, trace,
  or recolor the circular CMC beaver or the square chocolate Bober JPEG —
  this mark is original polygons only
- Same rounded-square badge + glow-circle recipe as any sibling
