HOW TO PLAY

Drag back to set angle. Charge power 0–100. Read the wind flag. Fire.

Hits dig the bank and hurt HP. Falloff at the blast edge. Creek and void below = out. Last beaver standing wins.

Crates drop every 3 turns. Walk onto one for Dynamite, Sap Bomb, or $BOBER.

Between matches, spend $BOBER on extra charges. Stick and Snowball stay infinite. You cannot buy a win.
