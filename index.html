HOW TO PLAY

Drag back to set angle. Charge power 0–100. Read the wind flag. Fire.

Hits dig cover and hurt HP (Bobers start at 85). Falloff at the blast edge. Creek, dust, void, methane, or acid below = out. Last beaver standing wins.

Crates drop every 3 turns. Walk onto one for gear or $BOBER. Tap SHOP mid-match to buy charges. If you are short, the shop says how many more $BOBER you need.

Ten maps: Lodge Bowl, Twin Ledges, Red Mesa, Crater Rim, Methane Shelf, Acid Vents, Ring Span, Deep Pack, Frost Pit, Dock Notch. Stick and Snowball stay infinite. Paid guns hit harder. Use the tray ◀ ▶ chevrons (or swipe) to reach Lodge Mortar, Pinecone Cluster, Woodchip Mine, Bark Buckler, Corkscrew Rocket, and Lodge Chaingun. You cannot buy a win.

Pick Easy, Normal, or Hard. After turn 12 — or when two Bobers remain — Sudden Death raises the hazard every 2 turns so the match ends.

Link Battle: Host gets a 4-letter room code. Friend taps Join and types it. Same Wi-Fi is best. Finding each other uses a short PeerJS internet hop — no account, no lobby. Host is Lodge, Join is Creek. Two devices.
